## Residual Risks

- Some cross-vertical operational cases are still flagged as barbershop/agendamento in random real-agent runs.
- Some mixed short follow-ups still inherit the wrong topic/tool in older scenarios.
- Tool-use labels can disagree with safe fail-closed textual answers.
- Naturalness still has P2/P3 repetition in sports lists and some acknowledgements.
- Real providers are still needed for live stock, delivery, appointment availability, weather/time windows, sports and news accuracy.

## Unlock Criteria For Any New Cycle

No new improvement cycle may start unless:

- `pnpm conversation:lab:rc` passes.
- Regression remains at or above 97% satisfaction.
- Regression P0/P1 do not increase from RC1.
- Real-agent P0/P1 do not increase from RC1.
- invented_data remains 0.
- no_leak remains 0.
- The proposed cycle has an explicit scope and forbidden-scope list.
- The cycle identifies which RC1 residual category it addresses.
