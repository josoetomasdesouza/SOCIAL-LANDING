## Main Improvements Captured

- Question Contract now resolves the real user question before response shaping.
- Conversational memory tracks useful topic, operational topic, tool topic, recent decisions, reactions, entities, answerable questions and offered next steps.
- Cross-vertical operational questions use fail-closed answers instead of inventing price, availability, stock, reservation or professional data.
- `que horas?` arbitration now considers active tool topic, explicit operational return, last entity and ambiguous tie cases.
- Internal terms are blocked by a final InternalLanguageGuard.
- Short non-operational messages such as `não entendi`, `não gostei`, `faz sentido`, `melhor não` and `e agora?` use recent useful context instead of opening a new operational flow.

## Known Residual Failures

- Some responses are correct but still sound macro-like or overly templated.
- Some expected-tool labels are stricter than the answer contract when a fail-closed text response is acceptable.
- Some old random scenarios still flag cross-vertical operational answers as barbershop/agendamento.
- Some short follow-ups such as `qual você escolheria?` still inherit sports in mixed contexts.
- Some P0/P1 findings require real provider/tool data rather than more heuristics.

## Baseline Rule

Future automatic improvement cycles must not increase P0 or P1 on the regression set, must not introduce invented data, must not introduce internal leaks, and must not reduce fail-closed discipline.
